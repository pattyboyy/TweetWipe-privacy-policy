# Privacy Policy for Tweet Wipe

Effective date: February 23, 2026

Tweet Wipe is a browser extension that helps users bulk-delete or filter their own X/Twitter posts from pages they open in their browser.

## Data We Process

Tweet Wipe can access:

- Post content and metadata visible on `x.com` and `twitter.com` pages you actively open.
- Extension settings you choose (for example: delay, filters, dry-run mode).

Tweet Wipe stores your settings using Chrome extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), which may sync across browsers signed into your Chrome profile.

## Data We Do Not Collect or Sell

Tweet Wipe does not:

- Collect personal data on our servers.
- Send your post content or account data to external services.
- Use analytics, ad tracking, or behavioral profiling.
- Sell or share your data with data brokers.

## How Data Is Used

Processed page data is used only to perform user-requested automation actions inside your current browser tab, such as:

- Finding matching posts.
- Triggering available delete/undo-repost actions.
- Displaying job progress and skip/error details in the extension popup.

## Data Retention

- Settings are kept in browser extension storage until you change them or uninstall the extension.
- Tweet Wipe does not maintain a remote database of user data.

## Security

- Tweet Wipe runs locally in your browser.
- The extension requests only the permissions required to operate (`storage`, `tabs`, `scripting`, and host access to X/Twitter domains).

## Your Choices

You can:

- Stop automation at any time using the extension popup.
- Use dry-run mode before running deletions.
- Remove extension data by uninstalling the extension in Chrome.

## Third-Party Sites

Tweet Wipe operates on X/Twitter web pages. Their terms, privacy policy, and UI behavior are controlled by X Corp and may change over time.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the Chrome Web Store listing for this extension.
